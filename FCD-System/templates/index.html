About Us

Welcome to our website for the Real-Time Fugitive Criminal Detection. Here, we delve into the innovative realm of utilizing cutting-edge technologies to address critical challenges in criminal detection. Our project aims to leverage the power of deep learning algorithms, real-time data processing, and intuitive user interfaces to create an advanced system that aids law enforcement agencies in identifying and apprehending fugitive criminals efficiently. Through a harmonious fusion of hardware and software, our solution aims to contribute to the enhancement of public safety and law enforcement efforts. Explore our website to discover the intricacies of our project, from methodologies and implementation to a result and future possibilities. Join us on this transformative journey at the crossroads of technology and security.

Features

Large Dataset of Fugitives

The system uses a large dataset of known fugitives and mugshots in Pakistan. This allows the system to identify fugitives with a high degree of accuracy.

User-Friendly Interface

The User-friendly interface makes it easy for law enforcement agencies to use. This allows them to quickly and easily identify fugitives and track their movements.

Updated Dataset & Model

Continuously updated Dataset to improve the Model's performance. This ensures that the system is always up-to-date with the latest information on fugitive criminals.

Fight Against Terrorism

The website makes it easy to do detection. This allows agencies to quickly and easily identify fugitives and track their movements.

Capturing Escaped Prisoners

The system can help in capturing escaped prisoners by identifying them using surveillance cameras and improve the security.

Identifying Repeat Offenders

The system can help to identify repeat offenders and track their movements. This can help to prevent them from committing further crimes.
